Calculation steps

Model & assumptions

  • Static balance as in the MATLAB reference: lift = weight (m0·g), split between the pairs by moment balance about CG. Buoyancy is deliberately excluded (handled separately).
  • Gust drag penalty: E[CL²] = CLtrim² + CLα²·σα², with σα from integrating the von Kármán spectrum in the frequency domain (fin-chord averaging, fore/aft delay, optional rigid-body response). Fixed-attitude mode reproduces the script.
  • Hull drag: ITTC-57 friction × Hoerner form factor on wetted area. Fin lift slope from AR and the selected 2D section (generalised Helmbold) unless calibrated; section CD0/CLmax from data/sections.json at the fin chord Re.
  • Waterjet momentum theory; ηjet converts engine power to jet-stream power. Range by Breguet with fuel burn (BSFC); equivalent to the script's V/c·L/D·ln(m0/mf).
  • Trajectory: linear pitch/heave and yaw/sway with added mass (Lamb), hull Munk moment, Froude–Krylov force; no steering.
  • Quasi-steady; CG fixed during fuel burn; trim evaluated at m0. Full derivation: docs/MODEL.md. All coefficients live in data/model.json.
